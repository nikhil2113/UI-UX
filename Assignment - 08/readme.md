# Final Conclusion Summary :

The comparative UX evaluation was conducted to assess the effectiveness of our modified high-fidelity Figma prototype compared with the original website. The study involved five participants who evaluated both versions using the Think Aloud method and provided feedback on their experiences.

The feedback from the participants indicated that the original website had several usability challenges, including difficulty understanding the registration process, confusion caused by multiple search options, difficulty identifying relevant profile details, and a lack of clarity regarding privacy settings and membership plans. In comparison, the redesigned prototype was generally considered more organized, easier to navigate, and more user-friendly. Participants appreciated the clearly visible search filters, improved match cards, accessible profile actions, better privacy explanations, and membership plans presented in separate cards for easier comparison.

Overall, the qualitative findings suggest that the redesigned prototype addresses several usability concerns identified in the original website. However, further improvements are needed to explain privacy controls, membership benefits, billing conditions, and renewal terms more clearly. The completed 18-parameter UX survey should also be analysed to quantitatively validate the improvements. Therefore, the prototype shows promising improvements based on participant feedback, while further testing and refinement are necessary to ensure a more intuitive, transparent, and accessible user experience.
